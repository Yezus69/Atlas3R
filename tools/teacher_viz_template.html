How to verify your home
  • Walk the cyan trajectory in your head — it should follow the path you actually walked.
  • Walls and furniture should appear as occupied (red) voxels where they really stand; doorways and walkable floor as free (green).
  • Use Measure on a span you can tape-measure (a door width is usually 0.8–0.9 m; a hallway width). The real value should fall inside the ±1σ band the tool prints. If it does not, the teacher's scale claim is wrong.
  • The voxel band only covers heights — a ~28 cm slab near the floor. Tall objects appear only by their lowest part.
  • Anything the camera never saw should be gray/unknown, NOT free. If unseen space shows up green, the teacher is lying.
  • Floor alignment FAILED on this scene (see red banner) — the slab is axis-aligned and may be tilted versus your true floor.
